# Section 3.3: Cross Beam Design (横桁の設計)

## Overview
- PDF pages: 675-764 (printed pages 670-759)
- Subsections: 3.3.1 through 3.3.7

## 3.3.1 Design Policy
- Cross beams handle load distribution and resist wind/seismic horizontal forces
- Load combinations: D+L, D+L+WS+WL, D+WS, D+EQ(L1), D+EQ(L2), construction
- Section force conversion: Mi = ML0 + (Mr0 - ML0) * (Li / L0)
- Pier cross beams: steel section only, no lateral buckling check needed

## 3.3.2 Main Frame Section Forces
- Load factors: DL 1.05, LL 1.25, construction 1.05
- 27 cross beam members (CR1-001 through CR1-027)

## 3.3.3 Wind and Seismic Effects
- Wind: V=40m/s, B=8.010m, D=8.110m, alpha=1.30, WS=40.06 kN/m
- Seismic L1: We=35.89 kN/m (Kh=0.25)
- Horizontal reactions: PR1 largest (Rw=2159kN, Re=1824kN)

## 3.3.4-3.3.6 Cross Beam Design
- Intermediate: CR1-026 rep, 73 groups; End pier: CR1-001 rep; Interm pier: CR1-009 rep
- Curvature additional force: W=-sigma*(Af+Aw/3)/R
- Section and bolt joint checks
